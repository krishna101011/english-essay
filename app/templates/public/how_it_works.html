How it works

  1. 1. Write or paste your essay

    Submit an essay or a book-chapter reflection. We autosave your draft as you type, so a lost connection or an accidental tab close won't cost you your work.

  2. 2. Instant local grammar check

    A local grammar and spelling checker runs first and flags spelling, punctuation, and basic grammar issues in seconds — no AI provider required for this step.

  3. 3. AI feedback on structure & vocabulary

    If you've connected an AI provider, it reviews sentence structure and word choice, and scores your essay against four rubric dimensions: grammar, vocabulary, structure, and clarity. Every suggestion is anchored to the exact text it refers to, so clicking a suggestion jumps you straight to it in the editor.

  4. 4. Apply, ignore, or revise

    Apply a suggestion directly, ignore it, or rewrite the passage yourself. When you resubmit, we compare your new version against the last one so you can see exactly what changed.

  5. 5. Track your progress over time

    Your progress dashboard shows your weekly average score trend and flags mistake categories that keep recurring — never from a single slip-up, only real patterns — with practice exercises generated from exactly those categories.

Curious about the grading itself? Read our feedback methodology, or view a sample feedback report before signing up.
