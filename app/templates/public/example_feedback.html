Inline corrections

My favorite hobby is gardening, it helps me relax after a long day at school. Last summer I planted tomatoes, peppers, and herbs in a small plot behind our house, it was alot of work but very rewarding. Watching something grow from a tiny seed into a plant I could actually eat felt really good, and it taught me patience I didn't expect to learn from a garden.

Sentence structure: gardening, it → gardening. It

This is a comma splice — two complete sentences joined only by a comma. Split them with a period, or join them with a semicolon or conjunction.

Sentence structure: house, it → house. It

Another comma splice — the same fix applies: end the first sentence before starting the next.

Spelling: alot → a lot

"Alot" isn't a word — "a lot" is always written as two words.

Vocabulary: really good → immensely satisfying

"Really good" is vague and overused. A more precise, vivid phrase makes the sentence more memorable.
