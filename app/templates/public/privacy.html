Privacy and AI data use

What we store

Your account email and password (hashed, never stored in plain text), the essays and book-chapter reflections you submit and every version of them, the corrections and scores generated for each version, any vocabulary words you save, your practice exercises and goal/streak settings, and — if you connect one — your AI provider API key, encrypted at rest and never shown back to you in full.

What your AI provider sees

If you connect an AI provider, submitting an essay sends its text (plus the local grammar checker's findings) to that provider's API, using your own API key, to generate sentence-structure/vocabulary suggestions and rubric scores. AI-generated practice exercises are the exception: they're built from category names only (e.g. "grammar", "vocabulary") — never your essay text.

Supported providers use fixed, HTTPS-only endpoints we control; a custom endpoint is only available where the operator has explicitly enabled it, and even then it must resolve to a public HTTPS address — never a local or internal one. Provider requests use your key directly; we don't inspect or log the content of the response beyond what's needed to save your corrections and scores, and error messages shown to you never include the provider's raw request details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your writing or personal data.
  • We don't run third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't send your API key anywhere other than the provider you configured it for.

Deleting your account

From Settings, you can permanently delete your account. This removes your essays and every version of them, corrections, scores, vocabulary words, drafts, goals, streak history, practice exercises, and AI provider settings. This action is immediate and cannot be undone, which is why it requires re-entering your password and typing your account email to confirm.

Security measures

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. API keys are encrypted at rest. Sessions use signed cookies. State-changing requests are protected against cross-site request forgery. Sensitive values (tokens, API keys, passwords) are redacted from server logs.

Questions about your data? See the Help / FAQ page, or reach out through the account you used to sign up.
