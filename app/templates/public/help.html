Help & FAQ

Do I need my own AI provider API key?

You need one to get sentence-structure/vocabulary feedback, AI rubric scores, model rewrites, and AI-generated practice exercises. The local grammar checker (spelling, punctuation, basic grammar) works without one. Add a key any time in Settings — most supported providers offer a free tier.

Is my writing kept private?

Your essays are stored in your account only, and we don't run third-party analytics or sell your data. If you connect an AI provider, your essay text is sent to that provider's API using your own key to generate feedback. See Privacy and AI data use for the full picture.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Verifying your email confirms it's really yours before you can start creating essays or chapters — you can still log in beforehand. If you didn't get the email, use "Resend verification email" on the editor page (there's a short cooldown between requests).

What does the "recurring mistakes" tracker mean?

It only flags a category (like punctuation or sentence structure) once it's come up several times recently — a single slip-up is never labeled a pattern. It's meant to point you toward practice exercises that actually matter for your writing.

Are the AI scores an official grade?

No — they're guidance to support revision, not an objective or authoritative grade. See Feedback methodology for what each score measures.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Settings. Deletion is immediate, permanent, and removes everything tied to your account. You'll need to re-enter your password and type your account email to confirm, since this can't be undone.

Can I export my essay and its feedback?

Yes — every submitted version has an "Export PDF" option that includes the essay text, rubric scores, and every correction with its explanation.
